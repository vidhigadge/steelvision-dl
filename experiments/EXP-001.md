# EXP-001 — Baseline CNN

## Objective

Train a small CNN from scratch as the first baseline for six-class steel surface defect classification.

## Model

Custom CNN with three convolution blocks.

## Input

224 × 224 grayscale images converted to 3 channels.

## Dataset Split

- Train: 70%
- Validation: 15%
- Test: 15%

## Hyperparameters

- Batch size: 32
- Epochs: 10
- Optimizer: Adam
- Learning rate: 0.001
- Loss: CrossEntropyLoss
