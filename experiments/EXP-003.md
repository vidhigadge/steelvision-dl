# EXP-003 — ResNet-50 Partial Fine-Tuning

## Objective

Evaluate whether fine-tuning the final ResNet-50 feature block improves steel surface defect classification compared with frozen-feature transfer learning.

## Model

ResNet-50 pretrained on ImageNet.

## Fine-Tuning Strategy

Frozen layers:

- `conv1`
- `layer1`
- `layer2`
- `layer3`

Trainable layers:

- `layer4`
- Final fully connected classification layer

## Input

224 × 224 grayscale images converted to 3 channels.

ImageNet normalization is applied.

## Hyperparameters

- Batch size: 32
- Epochs: 10
- Optimizer: Adam
- Learning rate: 0.0001
- Loss: CrossEntropyLoss
- Number of classes: 6

## Observations

- Partial fine-tuning produced rapid convergence.
- Validation accuracy increased from **91.60% in epoch 1 to 100% in epoch 2**.
- Training accuracy reached **100% at epoch 8**.
- Validation accuracy remained at 100% from epochs 2 through 9 before slightly decreasing to **99.60% at epoch 10**.
- Validation loss continued decreasing even after validation accuracy reached 100%, reaching its lowest value of **0.0037 at epoch 9**.
- The final epoch showed a slight increase in validation loss and a small decrease in validation accuracy.
- Test-set results are required before comparing EXP-003 conclusively with the frozen ResNet-50 experiment.

## Notes

Only ResNet-50 `layer4` and the final classification layer were fine-tuned.

A smaller learning rate of `0.0001` was used to avoid making excessively large changes to pretrained weights.

The test evaluation will be added after the fine-tuned checkpoint is saved and loaded successfully.
