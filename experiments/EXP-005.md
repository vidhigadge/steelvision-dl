# EXP-005 — Bicubic vs Real-ESRGAN at 64×64

## Objective

Evaluate whether neural super-resolution improves steel surface defect classification accuracy compared with standard bicubic upscaling at an effective input resolution of 64 × 64.

## Model

Fine-tuned ResNet-50 classifier from EXP-003.

## Input Resolution

Effective low-resolution input:

**64 × 64**

All images are ultimately resized to 224 × 224 before being passed to the classifier.

## Methods

### Bicubic Baseline

The original test images are:

```text
Original image
↓
Downsample to 64 × 64
↓
Upsample to 224 × 224 using bicubic interpolation
↓
Fine-tuned ResNet-50
```

### Real-ESRGAN

The original test images are:

```text
Original image
↓
Downsample to 64 × 64
↓
Real-ESRGAN x4 enhancement
↓
Enhanced image
↓
Resize to 224 × 224
↓
Fine-tuned ResNet-50
```

## Results

| Method | Test Accuracy |
|---|---:|
| Bicubic Upscaling | **49.80%** |
| Real-ESRGAN | **51.76%** |

## Accuracy Improvement

Real-ESRGAN improved classification accuracy by:

**+1.96 percentage points**

compared with bicubic interpolation.

## Observations

- Real-ESRGAN produced a small improvement over bicubic interpolation.
- Accuracy increased from **49.80% to 51.76%**.
- The improvement was limited to **1.96 percentage points**.
- The effect of super-resolution was highly class-dependent.
- `patches` achieved strong classification performance with an F1-score of **0.90**.
- `scratches` achieved very high recall of **0.97**.
- `pitted_surface` achieved high recall of **0.85**.
- `crazing` performance was very weak, with recall of only **0.02**.
- `rolled_in_scale` also performed poorly, with recall of **0.09**.
- These results indicate that Real-ESRGAN does not restore equally useful discriminative information for every defect type.

## Interpretation

The experiment shows that increasing visual sharpness does not automatically produce a large improvement in downstream defect classification.

Real-ESRGAN generates plausible high-resolution image structure, but this structure is not guaranteed to match the original fine defect details.

Therefore, neural super-resolution may improve visual appearance while providing only limited improvement in features that are important to the classifier.

## Research Finding

At an effective resolution of 64 × 64:


This represents a measurable but small improvement.

The result supports continued investigation but does not yet show that Real-ESRGAN consistently solves the low-resolution classification problem.
