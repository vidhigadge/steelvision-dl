# EXP-010 — Uncertainty and Reliability Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate whether the fine-tuned ResNet-50 classifier provides useful uncertainty signals and whether its predicted confidence scores are reasonably calibrated.

Two reliability measures were investigated:

- softmax confidence
- predictive entropy

Confidence calibration was also evaluated using Expected Calibration Error (ECE).

---

## Model

The experiment used the fine-tuned ResNet-50 classifier:

```text
models/resnet50_finetune.pth
```

The model predicts six steel surface defect classes.

---

## Test Dataset

The reliability analysis was performed on the full classification test set.

```text
Total test images: 255
```

Model performance:

```text
Correct predictions: 253
Incorrect predictions: 2
Accuracy: 99.22%
```

---

# Uncertainty Analysis

## Softmax Confidence

Softmax confidence was defined as the largest predicted class probability.

Higher confidence indicates that the model assigns most of its probability to one class.

---

## Predictive Entropy

Predictive entropy was calculated as:

```text
H(p) = -Σ p log(p)
```

Lower entropy indicates a concentrated probability distribution and therefore greater model certainty.

Higher entropy indicates that probability is distributed across several classes.

---

## Overall Results

```text
Mean confidence = 0.9471
Mean entropy    = 0.2083
```

---

## Correct Predictions

For the 253 correctly classified samples:

```text
Mean confidence = 0.9503
Mean entropy    = 0.2019
```

These predictions were generally characterized by high confidence and low entropy.

---

## Incorrect Predictions

For the two incorrect samples:

```text
Mean confidence = 0.5440
Mean entropy    = 1.0096
```

Compared with correct predictions, incorrect predictions had:

- substantially lower confidence
- substantially higher predictive entropy

This suggests that the model's uncertainty estimates provide useful information about prediction reliability.

---

## Incorrect Samples

The two incorrect predictions were:

### scratches_70.jpg

```text
True class: scratches

```

This was the most uncertain incorrect prediction.

### scratches_44.jpg

```text

```

Both classification errors occurred within the scratches class.

---

## Main Uncertainty Finding

Correct predictions showed:

```text
high confidence
+
low entropy
```

while incorrect predictions showed:

```text
lower confidence
+
higher entropy
```

This indicates that confidence and predictive entropy can act as useful warning signals for uncertain predictions.

However, only two incorrect predictions occurred in the test set, so this comparison should be interpreted cautiously.

# Calibration Analysis

## Purpose

A model is well calibrated when its predicted confidence approximately corresponds to its empirical accuracy.

For example:

```text
90% confidence
≈
90% accuracy
```

Calibration was measured using 10 confidence bins.

---

## Calibration Results

| Confidence Bin | Count | Mean Confidence | Accuracy | Gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.3–0.4 | 1 | 0.3963 | 0.0000 | 0.3963 |
| 0.4–0.5 | 3 | 0.4449 | 1.0000 | 0.5551 |
| 0.5–0.6 | 2 | 0.5575 | 1.0000 | 0.4425 |
| 0.6–0.7 | 9 | 0.6549 | 0.8889 | 0.2340 |

---

## Expected Calibration Error

The resulting Expected Calibration Error was:

```text
ECE = 0.0482
```

This corresponds to an average weighted confidence-accuracy mismatch of approximately:

```text
4.82 percentage points
```

---

## Calibration Interpretation

Overall model accuracy:

```text
0.9922
```

Overall mean confidence:

```text
0.9471
```

Because accuracy is higher than mean confidence, the model is slightly underconfident overall.

The majority of test images fall inside the highest-confidence bin:

```text
0.9–1.0
```

with:

```text
225 samples
mean confidence = 0.9817
accuracy = 1.0000
gap = 0.0183
```

This dominant confidence bin is therefore well calibrated.

The lower-confidence bins show much larger calibration gaps, but they contain very few samples.

---

# Reliability Interpretation

The model currently exhibits three useful reliability characteristics:

```text
Correct predictions
→ high confidence
→ low entropy

Incorrect predictions
→ lower confidence
→ higher entropy

High-confidence predictions
→ accuracy closely matches confidence
```

This suggests that uncertainty estimates may be useful for identifying predictions that require additional review.

---

# Practical Use

A future SteelVision-DL application could expose confidence and entropy alongside each prediction.

For example:

```text
Prediction: scratches
Confidence: 0.42
Entropy: high

→ flag for review
```

while:

```text
Prediction: patches
Confidence: 0.99
Entropy: low

→ model relatively certain
```

Any operational threshold would need to be validated on a larger and more representative dataset before deployment.

---

# Limitations

- The test set contains only 255 images.
- Only two test predictions were incorrect.
- Confidence and entropy are not guarantees of correctness.
- Softmax probabilities can still be misleading under distribution shift.
- The dataset may not represent real industrial operating conditions.
- The calibration analysis uses only the existing test distribution.
- No temperature scaling or other post-hoc calibration method was applied.
- Lower-confidence calibration bins contain very few samples.

Because of these limitations, uncertainty results should be treated as preliminary reliability evidence rather than a guarantee of safe real-world behavior.

---

# Main Finding

The fine-tuned ResNet-50 demonstrates a useful relationship between prediction correctness and uncertainty.

Incorrect predictions had substantially lower confidence and higher entropy than correct predictions.

The model also achieved:

```text
ECE = 0.0482
```

indicating relatively small overall confidence-calibration error.

---

Phase 10 successfully added uncertainty and calibration analysis to SteelVision-DL.

The classifier showed:

- high overall test accuracy
- lower confidence on incorrect predictions
- substantially higher entropy on incorrect predictions
- reasonably small calibration error
- slight overall underconfidence

These results provide a foundation for reliability-aware predictions in the later deployment pipeline.
